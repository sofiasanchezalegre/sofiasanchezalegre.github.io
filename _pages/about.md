Welcome to my personal website!

I am a Visiting Professor at the Business School of Universidad Torcuato Di Tella (UTDT).

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M) in 2026.

I am a macroeconomist with interests in **International Macroeconomics** and **Monetary Policy**, as well as the **Macroeconomics of Climate Change**.
